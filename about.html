Who We Are

Baked Fresh. Served with Love.

Chef's Cakes & Bakes is a local bakery serving delicious freshly baked cakes in Ejipura, Bangalore. We specialize in birthday cakes, anniversary cakes, photo cakes, and custom cake designs — made for every celebration, big or small.

Our goal is to bring joy to your special moments with cakes that look beautiful and taste absolutely amazing. Every cake that leaves our kitchen is crafted with the finest ingredients, shaped by skilled hands, and finished with the care your occasion deserves.

Whether you want a classic chocolate truffle, a dreamy red velvet, or a fully themed custom creation — we are here to make it happen, just the way you imagine it.
